# Decisions — match-rules

- **D1 Scoring.** Kill (`playerEliminated.byPlayerSlot` set and ≠ victim) +2, point orb +1, elimination with no credited other player (falling off alone, or a self-credited elimination) −1. All in `tunables.match.scoring`; set `fallPenalty` 0 to drop the penalty. Scores may go negative (no clamp: a clamp would make falling free for a player at 0). Knock-off credit reuses combat's `killCreditSeconds` (4 s); no second window.
- **D2 Point orbs live in a new `features/collectibles`.** Combat D2 recorded that point collectibles get their own feature. Launcher pickups stay in combat for now (moving them is a follow-up). Orbs spawn `dropHeight` above the authored point, fall with match gravity to that point and hover there (no terrain collision, so a blasted floor never strands one). Schedule mirrors combat D6: first after `firstDelaySeconds`, then every `intervalSeconds` while fewer than `maxActive`, round-robin over the level's `point-orb` collectibles, skipping a point that already holds an orb. No randomness in the sim.
- **D3 Respawn delay = out of play.** A level without spawns respawns players in the middle of its bounds. An eliminated player waits `respawnDelaySeconds` (1.2 s): not stepped, not hittable, cannot collect, not drawn, not a camera target. Steps 2 and 3 receive the active players only.
- **D4 Spawn choice.** Pick the level spawn maximising the distance to the nearest *active* opponent (feet points). Ties → lowest index. No active opponent → the slot's own spawn. Level spawns are author-placed safe points (GDD § 6); the engine does not judge hazards.
- **D5 Protection.** `invulnerableUntil = now + spawnProtectionSeconds` (2.5 s) on respawn; the player moves and fires freely (combat already only blocks damage, knockback still applies). Blink per DESIGN_LANGUAGE § 6: alpha 100 % / 45 % at 8 Hz plus a dashed ink ring. Round-start spawns are not protected.
- **D6 Round end.** At 0 the round ends; winners = every player with the top score (one → "P2 wins!", several → "Draw: P1 & P3"; nobody joined → "Time!"). Restart: any jump press once `restartLockSeconds` (2 s) have passed since the end; a fresh state on the same level and tunables.
- **D7 HUD on canvas.** Timer card and player cards are Pixi text/graphics in the `hud` layer with fixed canvas-ink colours (DESIGN_LANGUAGE § 7, simplified sizes). The control-hint line over the canvas uses canvas ink instead of the theme's muted foreground, which was near invisible in dark mode.
