# Decisions: material-sounds

- **D1** One sound per material, played on impact (dive wall bonk) and on destroy (blast). Alternative: separate `impactSound` / `destroySound` fields — a catalog schema change that collides with the parallel blast-power lap; deferred until a material needs two different sounds.
- **D2** The bonk keeps its body sound and layers the material sound over it, so the blob still sounds like itself; the owner's A/B decides which body sound (current dive impact or the landing splut).
- **D3** The bonked material is found by the match when it builds the sound snapshot (terrain cell just past the player in the dive direction), not by a new sim field, so dive and player sims stay untouched (game-sound D2: sound reads, the sim is unchanged).
- **D4** Existing IDs `thud` and `crumble` keep their meaning (stone and earth); cardboard and ice get new IDs. IDs are never renamed (LEVEL_FORMAT § 6).
- **D5** Owner picks (2026-11-16, listening page): stone take 4 (`thud`), earth take 3 + spare 1 (`crumble`), cardboard take 4 (`thwap`), ice take 2 + spare 4 (`clink`). Swap A/B: the wall bonk's blob part becomes the landing splut (`WALL_BONK_SOUND = "land"`); jump landings keep it. Dive hits on players keep `dive-impact`. Test batch: 16 takes, 32 credits.
- **D6** Volume follows intensity (owner rule, 2026-11-16): `intensityGain(speed, fullSpeed)` in `shared/config/gameJuice.ts` (floor `JUICE_VOLUME.minGain` 0.35, linear to 1). Two knobs in `SOUND_MIX`: `landingFullSpeed` 900 (fall speed) and `hitFullSpeed` 1000 (a hit victim's knockback speed, a bonk's speed into the wall; a full dive hit or blast is about 1000). Replaces `fullLandingSpeed` / `minLandingGain`. The material layer of a bonk takes the bonk's gain; a blast's terrain sound stays at full gain.
- **D7** Hit intensity is read from the victim's body speed in the frame the hit lands (the knockback), not from a new sim field, per game-sound D2.
