# Decisions: element-sounds

- **D1** Moment list and prompts as in BRIEF.md § Moments: 9 new sounds (6 short, 3 loops), the rest reuse existing cues or fade a loop. Frozen-blob moments (freeze solid, crack, shatter: the ice-frozen-blobs lap's option B "Ice block") are a later round.
- **D2** Generation (owner, 2026-11-18, option A): all 33 takes, `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`, `prompt_influence` 0.6, loops with the generator's `loop` option. ElevenLabs' estimate said 330 credits; the actual charge was 1.67 per 0.5 s take and 6.67 / 10 per 2 s / 3 s loop, **93 credits in total**. Flow "Bompers element sounds" (`lzVoMnamaJSTwfQVSwLa`) holds the originals.
- **D3** Processing as in the game (DESIGN_LANGUAGE § 8): shorts trimmed of leading silence, cut to 0.5 s with a 60 ms fade-out; loops kept whole with 10 ms edge fades; one fixed gain to −16 LUFS, limiter, mono 96 kbps mp3. The fire-crackle takes needed +18 to +24 dB.
- **D4** Listening page: https://claude.ai/artifact/BgTiAf9r1hezZHzAgTCKKx. Starting picks mark the take that needed the least gain (a proxy, not a listening judgement): torch-start 4, torch-loop 3, fire-crackle 1, burn-out 2, catch-fire 2, steam-hiss 4, freeze-start 3, freeze-loop 1, thaw 3.
- **D5** Owner picks (2026-11-18, listening page): torch-start 4, torch-loop 3, fire-crackle 1, burn-out 1, catch-fire 2, steam-hiss 3, freeze-start 3, thaw 2. Freeze loop: none of the first three; re-take as "a continuous mix of leaf blower and whistling wind" (3 takes, 20 credits, on the page as `freeze-loop-v2`). Pick pending; the code already holds the loop, it plays nothing until the file is in the bank.
- **D6** Held loops are a new engine capability (`setLoops`), not one-shot cues: the frame lists the loops it wants and the engine fades them in and out. Each refresh holds a voice for 0.25 s and schedules its own fade and stop, so pause, a closed match or a dropped frame go quiet without telling the engine (no change to the pause feature).
- **D7** One world fire crackle, not one per fire: its gain follows burning samples plus 60 per burning blob, full at 400 (`SOUND_MIX`). Starting values for the owner to tune.
- **D8** Burn-outs play fire's own `burn-out`: `reportTerrainDestroyed` takes a `soundId` override in place of `silent` (combat, one field), so the material keeps its own sound for blasts.
- **D9** Thaw and the torch's melt are read from the ice FIFO head and the terrain's frozen-flag version, at most every 0.5 s; no sim fields added (game-sound D2). Freezing a burning sample has no cue of its own: the freeze loop is already playing then.
