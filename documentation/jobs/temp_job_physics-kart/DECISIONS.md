# Decisions — kart

| # | Decision | By | Why |
|---|----------|----|-----|
| K1 | Owner picks (options page "Car Options"): Q1 go-kart only, 64 long, low and open, 2 blobs, springy, driver visible, variants as data lines; Q2 drivable (down to get in/out, left/right drive, jump hops, weapon from the seat, others push, ride and blast it); Q3 a moving kart pushes by weight and above a set speed takes one chunk like a headbutt, through combat's `applyHit`, never its own driver; Q4 engine hum from ElevenLabs (cap 2,000 credits). | owner | look and feel |
| K2 | A kart = two **wheels** (physics-objects round bodies, shape `wheel`, half the kart's weight each) + a **chassis** that is a spring-damped offset from the axle line (no body of its own against terrain). The wheels get motion, bumps, blasts, rockets, moving parts, fire, frost and pops from physics-objects with no new code; the chassis lags behind the axle's speed changes, which is the springy look (landing sinks it, a hop leaves it behind). | agent | reuse the ball's mechanism (README seam "the car"), smallest new physics |
| K3 | New feature `karts` (driving, seats, chassis, kart hits, kart render): a separate concept from round bodies (one reason to change: driving). Depends on physics-objects through its API. | agent | feature granularity, physics-objects stays under its file budget |
| K4 | Seated driver: kept in `stepPlayers` with its move, jump and crouch taken by the kart (aim and fire stay, so it fires from the seat), skipped by blob-vs-blob contacts and `freeStuckPlayers`, and placed on the seat after its step and after the kart's step. | agent | keeps aim/fire code untouched |
| K5 | Get in / out: a **fresh press** of down (the `crouch` input's press edge) while the blob touches the kart (in = any part of its body box over the chassis or a wheel, grounded or not; the kart empty, in play and not burning); press again to get out. The press that boards never crouches (the blob is seated that step), so crouch and crouch-roll (which need a held down on the ground) do not clash. Keyboard: the down key of the player's half; gamepad: d-pad down or the move stick pushed down. | agent | owner Q2, no new button |
| K6 | Getting out places the blob beside the kart on the side it faces away from the wheels' travel (then the other side, then above), each spot passed through the player's `escapeSolid` (the shared `freeSpotSearch`); it keeps the kart's speed plus a small hop. A burning kart puts its driver out (Q: "a driver jumps out when the kart burns"); a kart about to pop puts its driver out first. | agent | never-stuck rule |
| K7 | Kart hit threshold `karts.hitSpeed` = **380** units / s of closing speed along the contact (blob run speed is 340, kart top speed 520): a blob walking into a parked or slowly pushed kart is never hurt; a driven kart at speed is. One hit per blob per `karts.hitCooldownSeconds` (0.5 s). Source `kart`, kind `contact`; credit to the driver (null when empty). | agent | owner Q3: fair, one tunable |
| K8 | Blobs and the chassis: the chassis is never terrain to a blob. A blob on top (contact normal up) stands on it: pushed out up, carried along, given ground for a jump; from the side it is a mass-weighted bump on the wheels (the kart gives way first, with a terrain-safe move). | agent | owner Q2 "ride on it", never-stuck |
| K9 | Engine sound: two loops `kart-idle` and `kart-drive` crossfaded by speed (equal power), pitched per driver, silent when empty. The held-loop engine schedules crossfaded passes ahead, so one loop pitched by speed would step; two loops by gain is the common engine-sound layering. Owner picked take 3 of 4 single loops (ElevenLabs SFX, ≈ 27 credits of the 2,000 cap); the orchestrator made `kart-idle` = take 3 and `kart-drive` = take 3 resampled 1.4× (same motor, seamless crossfade). | agent + owner | owner Q4 |
| K11 | The kart model interfaces live in `karts/services/kartModel.ts`, not `types/`: they name physics-objects' `PhysicsObject` through its API, and dependency-cruiser lets a `types/` file import only other types folders (no cross-feature types path exists). Found in build (`pnpm arch:check`). | agent | layer rules unchanged |
| K12 | Not done in this lap (noted for the owner): a dive cannot headbutt a seated driver (drivers are out of blob-vs-blob contacts; the diver bumps the kart), balls collide with the wheels only (not the chassis), and a kart hits only blobs on foot. | agent | scope |
| K10 | Test level: Ball Playground gets 3 karts (not a new Kart Track): the long floors already drive well, karts knocking balls shows the interactions, and it needs no new library entry. | agent | owner asked to pick one |
