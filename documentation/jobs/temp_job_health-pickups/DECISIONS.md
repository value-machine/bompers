| D1 | Look: option A "Heart jelly" from the four-look options page (A heart jelly, B health tin, C heart bubble, D health capsule; A was recommended), made a **hard** jelly. | D2 | The health chunks become hearts: HUD card pips, over-head bar, and the chunks a hit throws off (they were pip-shaped). One shape (`heartPoints` in `shapeGeometry.ts`) and one draw (`shared/services/heartRenderer.ts` `drawInkHeart`, shared `inkStroke`). Filled hearts are the player colour, lost ones `healthLost`; same count and spacing logic. Over-head hearts grew from 6 × 5 to 7 × 6.5 world units and are never drawn under 9 px on screen, so they stay readable zoomed out. Chunk hearts are 11 × 10 design units. | owner (hearts), agent (sizes) | Closed |
| D3 | Heals +1 chunk (`collectibles.health.heal`), never above `combat.maxHealth`. Only a hurt blob in play may take it; a full-health blob passes through and it stays (the headbutt reset rule). | agent, owner accepted | Closed |
| D4 | Placed as `collectible` with `data-item="health"` (no format change). Catalog item `health`, taught in LEVEL_FORMAT and the level prompt with an example circle. 4 on Ball Playground: shelf, ramp top, pit, wall top. | agent (vision rule) | Closed |
