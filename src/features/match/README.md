# match

## Purpose

One round of the game (timed round, scoring, respawn and spawn protection, winner and restart — GDD § 5, § 6), and the composition root of the game features: the `GameState` shape, the fixed-step simulation `stepGame` (systems order), the render composition, the session hook that runs loop + input + render, and the `GameView` component the `/` page mounts.

Systems order in `stepGame` (new concepts slot into their number):

1. `joinSlots` — a connected input slot without a player gets one (the lobby's slot map leaves unclaimed slots disconnected, so only joined players spawn)
   1b. `stepMovingParts` — movers feature: moving parts take their pose for this step, then carry and push the players in play and the weapon crates (before anyone moves; respawns skip spawns a part covers, `openSpawns`); a body that stopped riding keeps the part's velocity once, capped (`GameState.moverCarry`, movers `inheritOnLeave`); each player's `squeeze` is set from the ride (null when free; the renderer's `squeezePose` squashes the body to fit and gives it the panic pupils, owner squish-eyes), and a player in a gap under `player.crushGapFraction` is crushed (`eliminateFighter`, cause `crush`); author-declared part sounds play at their cue points (`playPartSounds`, combat `partSound`)
2. `stepPlayers` — player feature; 2b. `resolveBodyContacts` — player feature (blob-vs-blob bounce among the players in play; its contacts feed 3b)
3. `stepCombat` — combat feature: rockets, blasts (damage, impulse, `clearCrater`); unarmed fire goes through its `unarmedAttack` hook to the dive's `requestDive`, a held torch through its `sprayAttack` hook to the fire feature's `sprayTorch` and a held freeze ray to the ice feature's `sprayFreezeRay`; 3e. `stepIce` — ice feature (ice-element lap 1): frozen samples thaw when their time is over (the ray itself freezes inside step 3, a fixed amount of ice per second on the nearest unfrozen ground to its beam); it runs before the fire step, so freezing puts fire out the same step; `GameState.ice` is created with the terrain, and the renderer draws frozen ground between the terrain and the fire and the rays' cold breath above the torch sprays (its frame carries the sim time for the grow-in and release); 3f. frozen blobs — ice feature (ice-frozen-blobs): before step 2 `frozenInputs` empties a frozen blob's inputs for the controller, combat and the dive (its jump presses crack the block); the ray chills only blobs that are not in a dive or spawn-protected (`canChill`); `stepFrozenBlobs` after 3e runs chill down and ends blocks on time; after 3d a blob that burns and carries ice loses both (`douseBlob` + `releaseBlob`); after 3b a `playerHit` of this step shatters the victim's block and `holdFrozenBlobs` writes the slide for the next step; the renderer passes the players in play and `ice.blob` to the ice layer and puts `frozenBlobPoses` over every other pose; 3c. `stepFire` — fire feature (fire-spread b1): the front spreads, burnt-out batches leave the terrain and are reported through combat's `reportTerrainDestroyed` at the batch's own debris spots (up to three, sound on the first only); `GameState.fire` is created with the terrain; 3d. `stepBurningBlobs` — fire feature (fire-burn b2): blobs catch, go out or pass it on; each burn tick (1 s, and 2 s unless crouching) takes a chunk through combat's `applyHit` (kind `burn`) and gets the player feature's `reactToHit` with no push (before step 2, the fire feature's `panicInputs` makes a burning blob with no move input run from the fire); 3a. `stepWeaponCrates` — collectibles feature (weapon crates drop, fall, land, get taken; combat's `takeWeaponPickups` arms the collector — right after combat, so a player a rocket eliminated this step cannot collect); 3b. `stepDives` — dive feature (the unarmed attack; hits go through combat's `applyHit`, a hit rolls the victim through the player feature's `reactToHit`; it gets this step's `inputs` so a dive held through its travel rolls on) Fire runs on every body of the arena (`fire.grounds`, mover-materials-l2): the match still calls `stepFire` once and reports its one merged batch; the fire loop's gain reads `burningCount(fire)`, parts included. Blobs on fire are the `blob-fire` feature with their own slice (`GameState.blobFire`, blob-fire-split): the torch hook passes the blobs `sprayTorch` reached to `catchFire`, step 3d calls `stepBurningBlobs(blobFire, { fire, … })`, and the renderer adds its layer above the torch sprays. Cold runs per body too (lap 2b): the sound snapshot reads `thawedCount(ice)` and the arena's `frozenVersionOf`, so thawing and freezing on a moving part are heard like on the ground.
4. `stepHeadbuttResets` — collectibles' `stepDiveRefills` with only the in-play players whose dive is spent (`isDiveSpent`); each taken one calls the dive's `refillDive` (headbutt-reset D2, D4)
5. `stepCollectibles` — collectibles feature: point orbs; each orb collected scores `match.scoring.orb`
6. `stepMatchRules` — leaving `state.killZone` emits `playerEliminated` (cause `out-of-bounds`, credit to the last hitter within `combat.killCreditSeconds`); new eliminations score; eliminated players wait `respawnDelaySeconds` out of play, then respawn at the spawn farthest from the opponents in play (a level without spawns: the middle of its bounds) with `invulnerableUntil = now + spawnProtectionSeconds`
7. `roundTimer` — at 0 the round ends, `winners` is fixed and steps 1–5 stop

Steps 2–4 receive only the players in play (`playersInPlay`): a player waiting to respawn, or eliminated earlier in the same step, is not moved, hit or able to collect.

Adding a concept: add its slice to `GameState`, its system call at its number in `stepGame`, and its render layer (create + sync pair from its feature api) in `gameRenderer.ts`.

## Decisions

- Only `match` knows `GameState`; other features take slices (game-engine-foundation D5).
- Beam clash (beam-clash D1): combat works out where held beams end each other; the match only passes the `reach` of the `sprayAttack` hook on to `sprayTorch` / `sprayFreezeRay`, hands sound the clash count, and empties `combat.beamClashes` when the round ends (combat stops then).
- Render the latest sim state without interpolation; revisit if motion stutters (game-engine-foundation D6).
- Fixed step 120 Hz, max 8 steps per frame; the backlog after a stall is dropped (`gameTunables.sim`).
- `applyLevelSettings`: a level's `matchDurationS` and `weaponIds` replace the lobby's `match.durationSeconds` and `match.weaponIds`; gravity multiplies (lobby D2, weapons-match-setting D1).
- Scoring: kill +2, point orb +1, an elimination nobody gets credit for −1; no clamp, all in `match.scoring` (match-rules D1).
- Kill zone: falling below the level bounds eliminates at once; the side and top kill lines lie `outOfBoundsMargin` = (jumpSpeed + strongest blast push + strongest weapon recoil)² / gravity beyond the bounds (twice the apex of a jump plus a point-blank blast and its recoil, jumps-and-recoil D2; the largest recoil of any weapon from combat's `maxWeaponRecoil`, one shot per launch, homing-missiles D5; with the match's effective gravity), so nobody notices them, even with chained jumps. Combat (rockets) and collectibles (crates) get the same zone as their `bounds` (open-map-edges D1–D3).
- Eliminated players are out of play for the respawn delay: not stepped, hit, drawn or framed (match-rules D3).
- Respawn at the spawn maximising the distance to the nearest opponent in play; ties → lowest index; level spawns are the author's safe points (match-rules D4).
- The match passes `player.crouch` to the player renderer and `crouchCount` into the sound snapshot (crouch lap).
- Spawn protection only blocks damage (combat), the player moves and fires freely; blink + dashed ring per DESIGN_LANGUAGE § 6 (match-rules D5).
- Round end: every top scorer wins (a draw lists them); restart by a jump press after `restartLockSeconds`; that press is consumed, so nobody jumps on the first frame of the new round (match-rules D6). A back press after the lock returns to the lobby (lobby D5).
- HUD drawn on the canvas with fixed canvas colours, never theme tokens (match-rules D7).
- A mid-air jump press is the dive's second input, so armed players can bash too (owner, double-jump-bash D1). The match wires no hook for it: the dive reads the controller's `airJumpPressCount` inside `stepDives`. Acceptance test: `gameSim.test.ts` § jump dive; the roll carry (D11, D13) stays inside `outOfBoundsMargin` (`matchRules.test.ts`).
- Built-in level routes are checked in the real sim (`gameSim.test.ts` § level routes): Bobsled Run's last gap (810) misses with a running jump + any dive (740) and is made by rolling down the ice chute and jumping out of the roll (owner, bobsled-run-roll).
- Test levels list terrain as `{ shape, materialId }` only: terrain has no role since level format 2 (material-system D5).
- The renderer hands the players view the barrel's shot pop (`feel.barrelPop`) next to the helmet spring; the pop itself is the player feature's (weapon-visuals-embellish D5).
- The player card shows the held weapon's ammo as a row of pills under the health chunks, in the weapon accent (the colour also names the weapon), hidden when unarmed; max ammo comes from one accessor (`maxAmmoFor`) so per-weapon ammo moves in one place (weapon-visuals D3).
