# match

## Purpose

One round of the game (timed round, scoring, respawn and spawn protection, winner and restart — GDD § 5, § 6), and the composition root of the game features: the `GameState` shape, the fixed-step simulation `stepGame` (systems order), the render composition, the session hook that runs loop + input + render, and the `GameView` component the `/` page mounts.

Systems order in `stepGame` (new concepts slot into their number):

1. `joinSlots` — a connected input slot without a player gets one (the lobby's slot map leaves unclaimed slots disconnected, so only joined players spawn)
2. `stepPlayers` — player feature; 2b. `resolveBodyContacts` — player feature (blob-vs-blob bounce among the players in play; its contacts feed 3b)
3. `stepCombat` — combat feature: rockets, blasts (damage, impulse, `clearCrater`); unarmed fire goes through its `unarmedAttack` hook to the dive's `requestDive`; 3a. `stepWeaponCrates` — collectibles feature (weapon crates drop, fall, land, get taken; combat's `takeWeaponPickups` arms the collector — right after combat, so a player a rocket eliminated this step cannot collect); 3b. `stepDives` — dive feature (the unarmed attack; hits go through combat's `applyHit`, a hard hit starts the player feature's tumble)
4. `stepHeadbuttResets` — collectibles' `stepDiveRefills` with only the in-play players whose dive is spent (`isDiveSpent`); each taken one calls the dive's `refillDive` (headbutt-reset D2, D4)
4. `stepCollectibles` — collectibles feature: point orbs; each orb collected scores `match.scoring.orb`
5. `stepMatchRules` — leaving `state.killZone` emits `playerEliminated` (cause `out-of-bounds`, credit to the last hitter within `combat.killCreditSeconds`); new eliminations score; eliminated players wait `respawnDelaySeconds` out of play, then respawn at the spawn farthest from the opponents in play (a level without spawns: the middle of its bounds) with `invulnerableUntil = now + spawnProtectionSeconds`
6. `roundTimer` — at 0 the round ends, `winners` is fixed and steps 1–5 stop

Steps 2–4 receive only the players in play (`playersInPlay`): a player waiting to respawn, or eliminated earlier in the same step, is not moved, hit or able to collect.

Adding a concept: add its slice to `GameState`, its system call at its number in `stepGame`, and its render layer (create + sync pair from its feature api) in `gameRenderer.ts`.

## Dependencies

- `level`, `arena`, `player`, `camera`, `combat`, `dive`, `collectibles`, `sound`, `pause` through their `api/` files (the level arrives in `MatchSetup` from the lobby).
- Shared engine: `gameLoop`, `gameInputService`, `usePixiApp`, `gameTunables`, `gamePalette`, `gameInput.types`.
- Mounted by `src/pages/GamePage.tsx` at `/` once the lobby starts a round.

## Decisions

- Only `match` knows `GameState`; other features take slices (game-engine-foundation D5).
- Render the latest sim state without interpolation; revisit if motion stutters (game-engine-foundation D6).
- Fixed step 120 Hz, max 8 steps per frame; the backlog after a stall is dropped (`gameTunables.sim`).
- Scoring: kill +2, point orb +1, an elimination nobody gets credit for −1; no clamp, all in `match.scoring` (match-rules D1).
- Kill zone: falling below the level bounds eliminates at once; the side and top kill lines lie `outOfBoundsMargin` = (jumpSpeed + strongest blast push + strongest weapon recoil)² / gravity beyond the bounds (twice the apex of a jump plus a point-blank blast and its recoil, jumps-and-recoil D2; the largest recoil of any weapon from combat's `maxWeaponRecoil`, one shot per launch, homing-missiles D5; with the match's effective gravity), so nobody notices them, even with chained jumps. Combat (rockets) and collectibles (crates) get the same zone as their `bounds` (open-map-edges D1–D3).
- Eliminated players are out of play for the respawn delay: not stepped, hit, drawn or framed (match-rules D3).
- Respawn at the spawn maximising the distance to the nearest opponent in play; ties → lowest index; level spawns are the author's safe points (match-rules D4).
- The match passes `player.crouch` to the player renderer and `crouchCount` into the sound snapshot (crouch lap).
- Spawn protection only blocks damage (combat), the player moves and fires freely; blink + dashed ring per DESIGN_LANGUAGE § 6 (match-rules D5).
- Round end: every top scorer wins (a draw lists them); restart by a jump press after `restartLockSeconds`; that press is consumed, so nobody jumps on the first frame of the new round (match-rules D6). A back press after the lock returns to the lobby (lobby D5).
- HUD drawn on the canvas with fixed canvas colours, never theme tokens (match-rules D7).
- Test levels list terrain as `{ shape, materialId }` only: terrain has no role since level format 2 (material-system D5).
- The player card shows the held weapon's ammo as a row of pills under the health chunks, in the weapon accent (the colour also names the weapon), hidden when unarmed; max ammo comes from one accessor (`maxAmmoFor`) so per-weapon ammo moves in one place (weapon-visuals D3).
