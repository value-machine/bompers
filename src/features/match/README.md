# match

## Purpose

One round of the game (timed round, scoring, respawn and spawn protection, winner and restart — GDD § 5, § 6), and the composition root of the game features: the `GameState` shape, the fixed-step simulation `stepGame` (systems order), the render composition, the session hook that runs loop + input + render, and the `GameView` component the `/` page mounts.

Systems order in `stepGame` (new concepts slot into their number):

1. `joinSlots` — a connected input slot without a player gets one (the lobby's slot map leaves unclaimed slots disconnected, so only joined players spawn)
   1b. `stepMovingParts` — movers feature: moving parts take their pose for this step, then carry and push the players in play and the weapon crates (before anyone moves; respawns and joining players skip spawns a part covers, `openSpawns`, level-orbit D4); a body that stopped riding keeps the part's velocity once, capped (`GameState.moverCarry`, movers `inheritOnLeave`); each player's `squeeze` is set from the ride (null when free; the renderer's `squeezePose` squashes the body to fit and gives it the panic pupils, owner squish-eyes), and a player in a gap under `player.crushGapFraction` is crushed (`eliminateFighter`, cause `crush`); author-declared part sounds play at their cue points (`playPartSounds`, combat `partSound`)
   1c. `freeStuckPlayers` — the never-stuck invariant (owner, never-stuck): no player starts its own step inside terrain or a part. The controller cannot move a body that already overlaps (every contact move backs off to where it was), so a body left overlapping (a part's push, a squeeze letting go, a part's edge it launched off) goes to the nearest free spot (player `escapeSolid`); where it fits only smaller it is squeezed for the step; where even the crush size (`player.crushGapFraction`) fits nowhere within a body width it is crushed (cause `crush`, with the part it is deepest in when there is one). `gameSim.test.ts` § never stuck fuzzes random parts (slide, spin, swing; thin to thick, slow to fast) by a wall and under a ceiling with random inputs and checks no player ends a step deeper in solid than contact noise
   1d. `holdToRespawn` — respawn on purpose (owner, never-stuck): holding `respawn` (Q / Backspace / pad Back) for `match.selfRespawnHoldSeconds` (1 s) without a break eliminates the player with cause `self`; `PlayerState.respawnHoldSeconds` counts it (raw inputs, so a frozen blob can bail out) and the renderer draws the fill ring (`respawnHolds`)
2. `stepPlayers` — player feature; 2b. `resolveBodyContacts` — player feature (blob-vs-blob bounce among the players in play; its contacts feed 3b)
3. `stepCombat` — combat feature: rockets, blasts (damage, impulse, `clearCrater`); unarmed fire goes through its `unarmedAttack` hook to the dive's `requestDive`, a held torch through its `sprayAttack` hook to the fire feature's `sprayTorch` and a held freeze ray to the ice feature's `sprayFreezeRay`; 3e. `stepIce` — ice feature (ice-element lap 1): frozen samples thaw when their time is over (the ray itself freezes inside step 3, a fixed amount of ice per second on the nearest unfrozen ground to its beam); it runs before the fire step, so freezing puts fire out the same step; `GameState.ice` is created with the terrain, and the renderer draws frozen ground between the terrain and the fire and the rays' cold breath above the torch sprays (its frame carries the sim time for the grow-in and release); 3f. frozen blobs — ice feature (ice-frozen-blobs): before step 2 `markShatterBonus` puts each frozen blob's `ice.blob.shatterDamage` into combat's `bonusDamage` (the next direct hit on it takes that much more, freeze-ray buff B) and `icedInputs` shrinks a chilled blob's run input and empties a frozen blob's inputs for the controller, combat and the dive (its jump presses crack the block); the ray chills only blobs that are not in a dive or spawn-protected (`canChill`); `stepFrozenBlobs` after 3e runs chill down and ends blocks on time; after 3d a blob that burns and carries ice loses both (`douseBlob` + `releaseBlob`); after 3b a `playerHit` of this step shatters the victim's block and `holdIcedBlobs` writes the slide for the next step; the renderer passes the players in play and `ice.blob` to the ice layer and puts `frozenBlobPoses` over every other pose; 3c. `stepFire` — fire feature (fire-spread b1): the front spreads, burnt-out batches leave the terrain and are reported through combat's `reportTerrainDestroyed` at the batch's own debris spots (up to three, sound on the first only); `GameState.fire` is created with the terrain; 3d. `stepBurningBlobs` — fire feature (fire-burn b2): blobs catch, go out or pass it on; each burn tick (1 s, and 2 s unless crouching) takes a chunk through combat's `applyHit` (kind `burn`) and gets the player feature's `reactToHit` with no push (before step 2, the fire feature's `panicInputs` makes a burning blob with no move input run from the fire); 3a. `stepWeaponCrates` — collectibles feature (weapon crates drop, fall, land, get taken; combat's `takeWeaponPickups` arms the collector — right after combat, so a player a rocket eliminated this step cannot collect); 3b. `stepDives` — dive feature (the unarmed attack; hits go through combat's `applyHit`, a hit rolls the victim through the player feature's `reactToHit`; it gets this step's `inputs` so a dive held through its travel rolls on) Fire runs on every body of the arena (`fire.grounds`, mover-materials-l2): the match still calls `stepFire` once and reports its one merged batch; the fire loop's gain reads `burningCount(fire)`, parts included. Blobs on fire are the `blob-fire` feature with their own slice (`GameState.blobFire`, blob-fire-split): the torch hook passes the blobs `sprayTorch` reached to `catchFire`, step 3d calls `stepBurningBlobs(blobFire, { fire, … })`, and the renderer adds its layer above the torch sprays. Cold runs per body too (lap 2b): the sound snapshot reads `thawedCount(ice)` and the arena's `frozenVersionOf`, so thawing and freezing on a moving part are heard like on the ground.
4. `stepHeadbuttResets` — collectibles' `stepDiveRefills` with only the in-play players whose dive is spent (`isDiveSpent`); each taken one calls the dive's `refillDive` (headbutt-reset D2, D4)
5. `stepCollectibles` — collectibles feature: point orbs; each orb collected scores `match.scoring.orb`
6. `stepMatchRules` — leaving `state.killZone` emits `playerEliminated` (cause `out-of-bounds`, credit to the last hitter within `combat.killCreditSeconds`); new eliminations score; eliminated players wait `respawnDelaySeconds` out of play, then respawn at the spawn farthest from the opponents in play (a level without spawns: the middle of its bounds) with `invulnerableUntil = now + spawnProtectionSeconds`
7. `roundTimer` — at 0 the round ends, `winners` is fixed and steps 1–5 stop

Steps 2–4 receive only the players in play (`playersInPlay`): a player waiting to respawn, or eliminated earlier in the same step, is not moved, hit or able to collect.

Adding a concept: add its slice to `GameState`, its system call at its number in `stepGame`, and its render layer (create + sync pair from its feature api) in `gameRenderer.ts`.

## Decisions

- Respawn on purpose (owner, never-stuck): scored like a fall — a hitter within `combat.killCreditSeconds` gets the kill, else it costs the player `scoring.fallPenalty`, so it never dodges a kill. Bindings are the menu's back keys, unused in play (Q, Backspace) and the pad's Back (View / Select), held 1 s so a stray press does nothing; it shows the normal knockout (goo, splat, pop).

- Only `match` knows `GameState`; other features take slices (game-engine-foundation D5).
- A frame that runs no sim step (a display faster than the 120 Hz sim) hands its jump / fire / crouch presses to the next frame (`mergeButtonPresses`), and the input reader reports a key tapped between two frames as held once, so a short press always reaches the sim (dash-feel lap).
- Beam clash (beam-clash D1): combat works out where held beams end each other; the match only passes the `reach` of the `sprayAttack` hook on to `sprayTorch` / `sprayFreezeRay`, hands sound the clash count, and empties `combat.beamClashes` when the round ends (combat stops then).
- Render the latest sim state without interpolation; revisit if motion stutters (game-engine-foundation D6).
- Fixed step 120 Hz, max 8 steps per frame; the backlog after a stall is dropped (`gameTunables.sim`).
- `applyLevelSettings`: a level's `matchDurationS` and `weaponIds` replace the lobby's `match.durationSeconds` and `match.weaponIds`; gravity multiplies (lobby D2, weapons-match-setting D1).
- Scoring: kill +2, point orb +1, an elimination nobody gets credit for −1; no clamp, all in `match.scoring` (match-rules D1).
- Kill zone: falling below the level bounds eliminates at once; the side and top kill lines lie `outOfBoundsMargin` = (jumpSpeed + strongest blast push + strongest weapon recoil)² / gravity beyond the bounds (twice the apex of a jump plus a point-blank blast and its recoil, jumps-and-recoil D2; the largest recoil of any weapon from combat's `maxWeaponRecoil`, one shot per launch, homing-missiles D5; with the match's effective gravity), so nobody notices them, even with chained jumps. Combat (rockets) and collectibles (crates) get the same zone as their `bounds` (open-map-edges D1–D3).
- Eliminated players are out of play for the respawn delay: not stepped, hit, drawn or framed (match-rules D3).
- Respawn at the spawn maximising the distance to the nearest opponent in play; ties → lowest index; level spawns are the author's safe points (match-rules D4).
- The match passes `player.crouch` to the player renderer and `crouchCount` into the sound snapshot (crouch lap).
- Spawn protection only blocks damage (combat), the player moves and fires freely; blink + dashed ring per DESIGN_LANGUAGE § 6 (match-rules D5).
- Round end: every top scorer wins (a draw lists them); restart by a jump press after `restartLockSeconds`; that press is consumed, so nobody jumps on the first frame of the new round (match-rules D6). A back press after the lock returns to the lobby (lobby D5).
- HUD drawn on the canvas with fixed canvas colours, never theme tokens (match-rules D7).
- A mid-air jump press is the dive's second input, so armed players can bash too (owner, double-jump-bash D1). The match wires no hook for it: the dive reads the controller's `airJumpPressCount` inside `stepDives`. Acceptance test: `gameSim.test.ts` § jump dive; the roll carry (D11, D13) stays inside `outOfBoundsMargin` (`matchRules.test.ts`).
- Built-in level routes are checked in the real sim (`gameSim.test.ts` § level routes): Bobsled Run's last gap (810) misses with a running jump + any dive (740) and is made by rolling down the ice chute and jumping out of the roll (owner, bobsled-run-roll).
- Headbutt vs direct rocket is checked on every shipped level's spawns (`gameSim.test.ts` § headbutt vs direct rocket): one headbutt and one rocket into a standing blob's middle knock it off from the same spawns and slide it as far (owner, bash-matches-rocket lap; both read `directHitPush` in `shared/utils/blastPower.ts`).
- Test levels list terrain as `{ shape, materialId }` only: terrain has no role since level format 2 (material-system D5).
- The renderer hands the players view the barrel's shot pop (`feel.barrelPop`) next to the helmet spring; the pop itself is blob-look's (weapon-visuals-embellish D5).
- The player card shows the held weapon's ammo as a row of pills under the health chunks, in the weapon accent (the colour also names the weapon), hidden when unarmed; max ammo comes from one accessor (`maxAmmoFor`) so per-weapon ammo moves in one place (weapon-visuals D3).
- Pickup juice (pickup-juice D1–D5): step 3a passes `state.collectibles.bursts` to `stepWeaponCrates` (a taken crate leaves a render burst, a taken orb leaves one in step 4); the renderer passes `feel.pickup` to the collectibles view and adds its `bursts` layer over the orbs; the HUD score pops 1.35 → 1 over 150 ms whenever it rises (an orb or a kill); the sound world snapshot carries `crateLandings` (`collectibles.crates.landCount`), and each player snapshot `orbCount` (`collectibles.orbCounts[slot]`, the orb chime, D8).
- The renderer builds the backdrop from the level's theme (`resolveBackdropTheme(level.settings.themeId)` from `backdrop-themes`, level-themes D1).
- Getting hurt (owner, hurt-feedback D1, 2026-11-20): the renderer lays the hurt pose (`hurtBlobPoses`) over every other blob overlay (burning hop, dive, squeeze) by multiplying its squash in; passes `feel.hurt` to combat (bar pop, low health) and `chunkSeconds` to effects; the combat `lowHealth` layer sits right under the players. The HUD pops every lost pip, not only one.
- Fixed pickups (health-pickups D3, D7): step 4 hands the level's fixed pickups to the `fixed-pickups` feature with a `canTake` rule (headbutt reset: dive spent; health: hurt, not eliminated) and applies what was taken (`refillDive`, combat's `healFighter`); its view layer sits beside the orbs.
- **Physics objects (physics-objects lap):** `GameState.physicsObjects`; step 1b carries balls on moving parts as riders (a band-sized circle at the ball's bottom; a pinned ball pops; `keepRelativeSpeed` stops a hopping ball stacking the part's speed), step 3 passes them to combat as `roundBodies`, step 3g (`stepObjects`, first in `stepElements`) runs `stepPhysicsObjects` with this step's explosions, sprays and rays; the renderer draws their layer above the collectibles; sound hears `objectSounds`.
- **Karts (kart lap):** `GameState.karts` (wheels live in `physicsObjects`). `movePlayers` runs the seats before step 2 (`stepSeats` with this step's iced inputs: a fresh down press gets in or out), gives seated drivers' stick, jump and down to the kart (`seatedInputs`; combat and the dive get those inputs, so a driver fires but never dives), seats them after step 2 and leaves them out of blob contacts and step 1c. `stepObjects` runs `driveKarts` before step 3g and `stepKarts` (3h) after it; `applyKartHits` turns kart hits into combat's `applyHit` (source `kart`, credited to the driver) and `reactToHit`. Rockets explode on the chassis (`kartRoundBodies` in `roundBodies`); the kart layer is drawn above the blobs (the driver sits in the tub); sound hears `kartEngines`.
- **Sleeping balls (#145):** the ride step calls `wakeObject` for a ball a moving part carried or pushed (`partMoved`: its rider body moved), so a sleeping ball follows its part.
- **Shipped-levels sweep without balls (ball-playground lap):** the headbutt-vs-rocket sweep strips physics objects from the shipped levels (a ball beside a spawn blocks one slide or catches the rocket); terrain objects stay.
