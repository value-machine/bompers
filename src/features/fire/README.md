# fire

## Purpose

Fire on the terrain field and the torch that starts it; blobs that catch it are the `blob-fire` feature (owner rules blast-power D4–D8, D18; laps fire-spread b1 and fire-burn b2). Fire is an arrival time per terrain sample: it spreads outward through touching fuel at the same speed every way, never across air, burns each sample away after a while, and scorches the earth it stops at. A blob touching burning terrain or the torch's flame burns for 2 s and loses two chunks over it (the second skipped when it crouches at the end), unless ice, a dive or a bump ends it first. Blasts never light anything (D4).

## How it works

- **Per body** (mover-materials D1, mover-materials-l2 D3): `FireState.grounds` holds one ground fire (`GroundFire`: front, lit, burn-out batch, scorch, version) per body of the arena, in the arena's `bodyGrids` order: 0 is the static terrain, then each moving part's own field. A fire spreads inside its body only (crossing where bodies touch is a later lap). On a part everything below happens on the part's field: the torch lights the sample it touches (`ignite` takes the body), the fire spreads at the same speed, a blob touching it catches (the blob's centre is mapped into the part's frame), and burnt samples are cut out of the part's field by the arena's `burnOut`, so the part's collision, outline and fill follow. One batch beat serves all bodies; a batch's debris spots are world points (`bodyWorldPoint`). The fire layers exist once per body; a part's sit in an arena pose layer, so its fire rides and turns with it (D4: on a spinning part the flames turn too). A fully burnt part is gone for the rest of the round, the same rule as terrain (owner, mover-materials-l2 D1, 2026-11-18). A frozen part behaves like frozen ground (lap 2b): the torch thaws it where the flame touches and lights only what is no longer frozen.

- **Fuel** is a material with `burns` (cardboard). A fuel sample with a `douses` sample (ice) among its 8 neighbours never catches (`canBurn`). Both flags come from the content catalog through the arena's cell slots. 
- **Spread** (fire-spread D4, D9): lighting a sample at time t queues each touching fuel sample at t + step / `fire.spreadSpeed` (step = one sample or a diagonal), keeping the earliest time (lazy decrease-key heap, ties by index so the sim stays deterministic). "Touching" is the 8 neighbours, but a diagonal step needs a solid sample beside it, so fuel meeting only at a corner of air does not pass fire. A breakable non-fuel neighbour (earth) is marked `scorched` and stops the front; stone simply blocks, so fire flows around it.
- **Burn-out:** a lit sample burns `fire.burnSeconds`, then waits for the next batch, applied every `fire.burnOutInterval` through the arena's `burnOut` (one writer; the outline and collision follow). `stepFire` returns the batch with up to three `spots`: centres of samples of the batch itself, picked evenly through it, one per 16 burnt samples. The match reports the batch as combat `terrainDestroyed` events at those spots (cardboard's dust), with the sound (`thwap`) on the first only, so a big fire throws debris where it actually burns out and still makes a few cues a second, not one per sample. One centre for the whole batch put the debris where the fire started, since a fire burns out along a front (owner, 2026-11-17).
- **Torch** (blast-power D8; fire-spread D5–D7): while its holder holds fire with fuel left, combat calls the `sprayAttack` hook every step and drains `dt` seconds of fuel (`combat.torch.ammo` = 3 s). `rays` rays fan over ±`halfAngleDegrees` of the aim from the helmet, marching half a sample at a time up to `length`; each stops at the first terrain and lights it when it is fuel. Blobs a ray reaches before terrain get `push` units / s² along the aim on their velocity (like recoil, not through `reactToHit`), at most √(2 · push · length) ≈ 390 units / s, under a blast's push. A ray also stops on a moving part (the arena's `terrainBodies`): the flame ends there and the blob behind it is not pushed or lit. The part itself takes no fire yet (mover-materials lap 1; lap 2 lights it).
- **Look ("Sticker flames", owner fire-visuals D1–D3):** every flame is drawn in ink first (grown by the effect outline width) and in colour on top, so a fire has one ink line around it. On terrain (owner fire-clusters D1, "Big flames"): burning samples are grouped in 2 × 2 blocks and touching blocks are drawn as one fire shape: an ember body slab per row run of blocks (each overlapping the rows beside it), amber inside where a block in the first 60 % of its burn has fire on both sides and below, and flames on the top edge only. A flame on an even block column takes in the flames beside it and grows up to 1.9 × wider and 1.6 × taller; every flame is 8 % taller per burning block around it (up to 5) and taller toward the middle of its cluster (0.91 × alone, 1.3 × four blocks in), with its own fixed height and width from its place on the grid; a flame is full size 0.12 s after catching and shrinks to 35 % by burn-out (taller where the sample above is air). Neighbours count in as they flare up, so nothing pops. The body covers burning cardboard, so the char tint of fire-visuals D1 is gone (D3). A big fire costs a body slab per row run plus its top-edge flames, not a flame per block; scorch circles on scorched earth (redrawn only when `version` changes); a white smoke puff with an ink edge where a batch burns out (at most 24 alive). The spray starts at the nozzle (15 units along the aim), its neck opens to 45 % of the hit cone and swells into a head of six rolling lobes, lifting a little toward the end; five flames peel off the top and float up; a ray that met terrain gets a flare; the flame grows in over 0.12 s and, on release, its head lets go as one puff (0.3 s). A held torch that is not spraying shows a small flame on its nozzle. The shapes are fitted inside the rays the sim cast, so the flame never shows more than the torch lights. A burning blob (owner D1: the options page's variant 2 with variant 1's drawn hops) hops in place on the drawing only (`lift` overlay, a light squash at each touch-down; the body and its shadow stay on the ground), has pinprick pupils, three flames on its helmet and sweat drops flicking off; its moments show for `fire.blob.momentSeconds`: a flame pop on catching, white steam off ice, flames blown back by a dive, a flame ball arcing to the bumped rival, grey smoke when it burns down.

## Dependencies

- `arena` via `api/arenaApi.ts` (`TerrainGrid`, `EMPTY_CELL`, `burnOut`, `ClearedCells`, `bodyGrids`, `terrainBodies`, `bodyWorldPoint`, `createPoseLayer`, `placePoseLayer`).
- `combat` via `api/combatApi.ts` (`helmetPoint`); combat itself does not import fire (the torch arrives through the `sprayAttack` hook; burn damage is applied by the match).
- `player` via `api/playerApi.ts` (`PlayerState`, `BodyContact`, `BlobPoseOverlay`).
- Shared: `gameTunables` (`fire`, `fire.blob`, `combat.torch`), `gamePalette.fire`, `gameGeometry.types`, `gameInput.types`; `pixi.js` in the renderer.
- Consumed by `match` (state slice, step 3c, the hook, two render layers, sound counters) and by `blob-fire` (reads `FireState.grounds` to catch from burning ground).

- `src/shared/utils/debugInk.ts`: the colour of each ink pass (body ink, flame ink, spray ink, scorch). With `?debugInk=1` in the URL each one is drawn in its own colour, to tell which layer a flashing black shape belongs to (fire-black-flash diagnosis).

## Decisions

- Shared with ice (ice-element D4, D6): the front itself is the shared sample front (`src/shared/utils/sampleFront.ts`; `FireState.front`, with `igniteAt` as its arrival times), the torch's ray fan is the shared `castRayFan` (`src/shared/utils/rayFan.ts`), and the fire's body is drawn by the arena's region contour, as frozen ground is (element-overlay-shape D1). A frozen sample can't burn, so a spreading fire stops at it and does not return when it thaws; a burning sample that freezes goes out and stays standing (`douseFrozen`, run when the terrain's `frozenVersion` changes). A torch ray that meets frozen terrain thaws everything within `ice.torchThawRadius` of it instead of lighting it; the next step lights it if it is fuel.

- Beam clash (owner, beam-clash D1, the "Steam wall"): a freeze ray that meets the flame ends it there. `sprayTorch` takes the `reach` combat worked out (`src/shared/utils/beamClash.ts`) and casts its fan no further, so the flame lights, pushes and is drawn only up to the meeting point. This feature knows nothing of the other beam.
- Fire pace (owner, fire-clusters D5, 2026-11-17: "spread way slower and destroy waaaay slower"): `fire.spreadSpeed` 13 units / s (was 40) and `fire.burnSeconds` 7.5 s (was 1.5), starting values the owner tunes from. The burning band is speed × burn ≈ 98 units deep (was 60). Blob burn, the torch and the burn-out batch beat are unchanged; the sim tests take their run times from the tunables.
- Fire clusters (owner, fire-clusters D1, 2026-11-17): "Big flames" from the options page (over a joined crown and a bonfire peak), plus the owner's additions: taller toward the middle of the burn and a bit of randomness in size, as long as it costs no frames (it adds no shapes). The merged outline is body slabs plus top-edge flames under one ink pass, not a per-frame contour of a heat field, which would re-contour and re-tessellate the whole fire every frame (D2). The fire body replaces the char darkening (D3).
- Fire look (owner, fire-visuals D1–D3, 2026-11-17): "Sticker flames" over gumdrop puffs, paper burn and lava bubbles; the spray billows and sheds flames upward on a rolling-cloud head with a half-cone neck, and the hit area stays straight and unchanged. The burning-blob look below is untouched (D4).
- Lap cut (owner, fire-spread D1): b1 = torch + fire on terrain; b2 = blobs catching fire (2 s, then −1 chunk; ice or a dive puts it out; a bump passes it on), with the burn state kept in this feature.
- Fire sounds (owner picks, element-sounds D5; were placeholders under fire-spread D8 / fire-burn D7): the torch start `torch-start` and a held `torch-loop`, a world `fire-crackle` loop by how much burns, burn-outs fire's own `burn-out`, a blob catching fire `catch-fire`, ice putting it out `steam-hiss`. The sound feature reads them from `sprays`, `lit` / `litHead`, `blobs` and the counters; this feature emits nothing for sound.
- The torch flame leaves just inside the rim of the torch's funnel mouth: `FLAME_LOOK.spray.nozzleReach` is 18 units along the aim from the helmet point and follows the shared `weaponTube` funnel shape (weapon-visuals-embellish D1, D8).
- Body in the terrain's shape (owner, element-overlay-shape D1, 2026-11-18, "C by far", options page https://claude.ai/artifact/PhbxM4HmnA9yWHLoDX74ox): a fire's body is the terrain's own contour limited to the burning samples (`markedRegion` + `terrainContour` from the arena), in ink grown by the outline width, in ember, and the fresh samples' inside in amber (the same contour, inset by `FLAME_LOOK.cluster.hotInset`). It no longer sticks out past the terrain and has no row pieces; its edge inside the terrain is a curve. Flames are rooted on the terrain's edge (`edgeAbove`), `flameRoot` under it. The body is redrawn when `fire.version`, the terrain's `shapeVersion` or the outline width changes, and four times a second for the hot inside; only the flames are redrawn every frame. Rejected on the page: clipping the row pieces to the terrain, staining the terrain's own fill, rounding only outer corners.
- Blob fire split out (blob-fire-split, 2026-11-18): burning blobs (`burningBlobs`, `blobFireRenderer`, their state) are the `blob-fire` feature, a second domain concept that had this feature at its file budget. `sprayTorch` returns the blobs its flame reached and the match sets them alight, so this feature does not import `blob-fire`; `blob-fire` reads this feature's `FireState.grounds` through the API. Behaviour is unchanged.
