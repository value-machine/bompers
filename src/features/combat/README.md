# combat

## Purpose

Fighting (GDD § 2 Rocket Jumping, § 3): four health chunks per player, the shared hit / damage API (front / back zones, damage from tunables), the unarmed-attack hook, the rocket launcher and the homing launcher (their crates drop in the `collectibles` feature; combat arms whoever takes one), rockets, explosions (damage to others, impulse to everyone, craters), the spawn-protection hook, and the typed sim events other systems read. Runs as sim step 3 in `match`.

Seams for later work:

- **Match rules:** set `FighterState.invulnerableUntil` (sim seconds) on respawn; replace match step 4; read `playerHit` / `playerEliminated` from `combat.events` (each consumer remembers the last `seq` it read; events live `eventRetentionSeconds`).
- **Dive** (`features/dive`): match passes its `requestDive` as `unarmedAttack`; the dive deals damage with `applyHit({ kind: "contact", source: "dive", contactPoint, direction })`; knockback is its own.
- **Hurt reaction:** an explosion calls the player feature's `reactToHit` with the impulse it gave each other player; the victim rolls as hard as the blast pushed it (a close, strong blast sends it flying and bouncing; the edge of the range only shoves; player `roll.ts`). The shooter is only pushed, never rolled, so rocket jumps stay in control (dive-headbutt D10).
- **Sound:** event `type` → SFX (`rocketFired`, `explosion`, `playerHit`, `playerEliminated`, `pickupCollected`); `terrainDestroyed.soundId` / `effectId` come from the catalog material.
- **Effects:** explosion, impact pop and debris live in the `effects` feature (reads `combat.events`). The rocket tube is drawn by the player renderer, as part of the blob's silhouette.
- **More items:** a new id in `WeaponItemId` (`src/shared/types/weaponItems.types.ts`) and `src/shared/config/weaponItems.ts`, its `MODE_OF` row in `weapons.ts` (and a `KIND_OF` row if it fires rockets) and a tunables section; if it flies differently, a new `RocketKind`; a held weapon that is not a rocket is a `spray` weapon behind the `sprayAttack` hook (the torch, fire-spread b1).
- **Terrain gone:** `reportTerrainDestroyed(combat, { cleared, at, timeSeconds })` (in `explosions.ts`) turns any `ClearedCells` into `terrainDestroyed` events (one per material + colour, with the catalog effect / sound); blasts use it, and match uses it for fire burn-outs. Its crate drops in `collectibles` (combat D2: pickups moved there); the catalog item makes it listable in a level's `data-items`.
- **Tuning:** every number (also the crate drop schedule, `combat.drops`, read by `collectibles`) is in the `combat` section of `src/shared/config/gameTunables.ts`.

## Dependencies

- `player` (`PlayerState`, `playerBody`), `arena` (`clearCrater`, `isSolidAt`, `rectHitsSolid`), `level` (`LevelDrop`), `content-catalog` (material effect / sound) through their `api/` files.
- `pixi.js` (renderer only), `gameTunables`, `gamePalette`, shared geometry / input types.
- Consumed by `match` (state slice, step 3, step 4 hooks, render layers).

## Decisions

- Combat state is a `GameState.combat` slice keyed by player id; `PlayerState` only carries motion (combat D1).
- Events carry a monotonic `seq` and are pruned by age, so several sim steps per frame never hide one (combat D3).
- Elimination flags the fighter; match step 4 does the respawn (combat D4).
- Launcher: 6 rockets, no time limit, dropped when empty or on elimination (combat D5).
- Drops: first after 3 s, then every 7 s while fewer than 2 lie in the arena, round-robin over drop points — the sim has no randomness source (combat D6). The crates themselves live in `collectibles` since the pickups-to-collectibles lap (D1 there).
- Each weapon names the crater shape its blast cuts (`craterShape`, `src/shared/types/craterShape.types.ts` + its distance rule in `src/shared/utils/craterShape.ts`); rocket and homing are `"round"`. A new shape is a new union member plus its rule (terrain-field D5, owner blast-power D17).
- One blast power per explosion (0–100, each weapon's `power`; rocket and homing 50) sets everything in straight lines: reach `reachPerPower · P`, centre push `pushPerPower · P` falling off to `minFalloff`, crater reach `craterPerPower · P` ÷ the material's toughness (`combat.blast`; blast-power D1, D3, D10). Power 50 reproduces the pre-power blast (push 1080, reach 64, crater 34). Distance is measured to the nearest point of the body box (combat D8). Replaces combat D7 (separate push / crater radii).
- The blast hands each pushed player one `{ direction, impulse }` through `reactToHit`; the roll strength lives in the reaction, so the shared-roll lap replaced the tumble threshold without touching blast code (blast-power D12, shared-roll D7).
- Knockback and impulses still apply during spawn protection; only damage is blocked.
- Every shot has recoil (`combat.rocket.recoil`, applied by `fireRocket` through `kickShooter` in `rockets.ts`, kept apart from the rocket's touch / bounds logic): an instant velocity change opposite the aim, on the ground and in the air, so firing is also movement (down-shots hop or slow a fall, level shots push back, mostly felt in the air). An upward kick leaves the ground. It stacks with a rocket jump's blast, so match's `outOfBoundsMargin` counts it in the fastest launch (jumps-and-recoil D2). The blast impulse was scaled down with gravity (D1 in the player README) while `tumble.minImpulse` stayed, so only close blasts (within about a quarter of the radius) tumble victims (jumps-and-recoil D3).
- Every attack deals damage through `applyHit`; how much is the `combat.hitDamage` table by hit kind (`contact`, `blast`, `burn`) and zone (`front`, `back`), never per-attack code. Defaults: contact 1 / 2, blast 1 / 1 (combat D10, owner scope change), burn 1 / 1 per tick (a burning blob, source `fire`; two ticks per burn, fire-burn D5, D10).
- A crouching victim (`PlayerState.crouching`) takes `floor(damage × combat.crouchDamageScale)` (0.5): halved, rounded down, so a 1-chunk hit deals 0 and still lands (owner feedback round 2 § 2.4; crouch D1, D2). A `burn` hit is not halved: ducking doesn't stop fire (fire-burn D5); crouching instead skips a burn's second tick, decided in the fire feature (D10). One step in `applyHit` via `crouchDamage`; knockback and the tumble reaction are unchanged.
- Homing launcher (owner request 2026-11-16, "homing but dodgeable"): a second weapon whose missiles are rockets of kind `homing` in the same list and flight path (homing-missiles D1). Dodge rule (D2, numbers in `combat.homing`): target locked at launch = the nearest live opponent within 70° of the aim (none → straight); 0.2 s straight before steering; 480 u/s (1.4 × run speed) with at most 2.6 rad/s of turn, a turn radius of ≈ 185 units, so a late jump or sidestep makes it overshoot; steering fuel 1.6 s, then straight; air-burst at 3 s; same blast as a rocket. 3 missiles per crate, 0.7 s cooldown, recoil 120 (D3). `rocketFired` carries `kind`.
- Combat has no unarmed attack of its own: the dive lap provides it, plugged in through the `unarmedAttack` hook (combat D11, owner scope change).
