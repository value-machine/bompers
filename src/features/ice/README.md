# ice

## Purpose

Frozen ground on the terrain field and the freeze ray that makes it (owner, ice-element D1–D6; lap 1 of the ice element). It is the mirror of the fire feature on the same system: the cold is an arrival time per terrain sample on the shared sample front (`src/shared/utils/sampleFront.ts`), and a frozen patch is drawn as the same kind of shape as a fire's body. The difference is what feeds it: fire feeds itself through fuel and runs on time; the cold is fed only by a freeze ray on terrain and runs on distance from the beam, a fixed amount of ice per second. The ray also freezes blobs: half a second in it puts a blob in an ice block for 2.5 s (lap 2, ice-frozen-blobs D1).

## How it works

- **Frozen is a flag, not a material.** The arena keeps `TerrainGrid.frozen` per sample (`setSampleFrozen`, `thawAround`). While set, the sample keeps its material and colour but behaves like ice in the arena's own queries: it grips with the ice material's friction, blasts do not cut it, and fire cannot enter it. This feature decides when the flag is set and cleared. **Per body (mover-materials lap 2b):** every body of the arena freezes on its own field: `IceState.grounds` holds one thaw queue (`GroundIce`) per body in the arena's `bodyGrids` order (0 = the static terrain, then each moving part), and the frozen flag is that body's grid's. The freeze ray feeds the body each ray touches (`feedCold` takes the `body`; a fan split over bodies splits its ice by the rays on each, so a step never makes more), one feed per ray and body, so moving the beam from the ground onto a part starts a new patch there. The cold stays inside the body it was made on. On a part the frozen patch grips like ice where it is frozen (the arena reads the part's samples under the feet), fire cannot enter it, freezing a burning part sample puts it out, the torch thaws it, and it thaws by itself after the same time. The frost layers exist once per body; a part's sit in the arena's pose layer, so the ice rides and turns with the part. `thawedCount(ice)` (thawed queue entries over all bodies, only goes up) feeds the thaw sound.
- **The cold flows from the beam** (owner, D3, D8): every second of beam on the ground makes the same amount of ice (`ice.freezeAreaPerSecond`, 3600 units²), and each bit lands on the nearest ground that is not frozen yet, measured through solid ground from the samples the beam touches. Fresh ground gets a round patch; a beam held inside a frozen patch pushes out the patch's nearest edge; frozen ground the beam is not near never moves. `feedCold(ice, grid, { slot, samples, now, dt, tunables })` does it once per ray per step: it adds the step's ice to the ray's budget (fractions carry over) and runs the shared sample front on distance instead of time (`advanceFront` with `until`, the same front fire spreads on; 8 neighbours with the corner rule, never across air), walking over frozen samples for free and freezing unfrozen ones until the budget is spent. Each player slot has its own feed (`IceState.feeds`); its distances start over when the beam touches other samples or comes back after a pause. Ice terrain (`douses`) is never entered or crossed. A step's search walks at most 4000 samples, so aiming at the middle of a very large patch costs a bounded amount and carries on next step. Let go and nothing grows. Which ground carries cold is the material's cold reaction (`reactions.cold`: `freezes` or `blocks`, the material × element table, mover-materials lap 3); ice terrain blocks.
- **Thaw** (D5): a frozen sample thaws by itself `ice.frozenSeconds` after it froze (a FIFO, as fire's burn-out; `stepIce`). The torch's flame thaws sooner (fire feature, `thawAround`); a sample frozen again gets a new entry and the old one is skipped.
- **Fire** (D6): a spreading fire stops at frozen ground and does not come back for it when it thaws. Freezing a burning sample puts it out (the fire feature drops it when `frozenVersion` changes); it stays standing. The torch always thaws frozen ground first and lights it on a later step only if it is fuel. Match runs `stepIce` before `stepFire`. Fire now keeps one ground fire per body (`fire.grounds`, mover-materials-l2); the tests here that light fire name the body (0 = the static terrain). Cold on moving parts is the next lap (2b).
- **Freeze ray** (D1): combat calls the `sprayAttack` hook every step its holder holds fire and drains `dt` seconds of charge (`combat.freezeRay.ammo` = 3 s). The fan is the shared `castRayFan` (`src/shared/utils/rayFan.ts`), the torch's too: `rays` rays over ±`halfAngleDegrees` up to `length`, each stopping at the first terrain. Blobs the beam reaches first get `push` units / s² along the aim. A ray also stops on a moving part (the arena's `terrainBodies`): the breath ends there and the blob behind it is not pushed or chilled. The part itself takes no cold yet (mover-materials lap 1; lap 2 freezes it).
- **Look:** frozen ground in the game's ice colours (`gamePalette.ice`, the `frost` swatch): the terrain's own contour limited to the frozen samples (the arena's `markedRegion` + `terrainContour`, as a fire's body; owner, element-overlay-shape D1, 2026-11-18), a lighter lip one sample deep along every top edge (the same contour without its top sample row drawn over it), a white gloss streak on about one block in nine inside the patch. It lies inside the terrain's own ink line. The ray is "Cold breath" (owner, D9): the torch's twin in the ice colours, drawn in ink first and in colour on top. A thin neck leaves the emitter (15 units along the aim), flares and lifts a little toward the end, and ends in a head where the breath lands: a round heart wider than the neck's end, so the neck's corners never show (owner, 2026-11-18), with six lobes rolling around it; six white snow specks drift down under it. It grows in over 0.12 s and, on release, the head lets go as three puffs that rise and shrink (0.3 s). It follows the middle ray the sim cast, so it lands where the ice does; the head is wider than the narrow line the ray actually hits.
- **Frozen blobs** (owner, ice-frozen-blobs D1, the "Ice block"): a blob the beam reaches before terrain gathers chill, `dt` per step and half of that while crouching; out of the beam the chill runs back down at the same speed, so a sweep does nothing. At `ice.blob.freezeSeconds` (0.5 s) it is frozen solid for `frozenSeconds` (2.5 s). The match leaves out blobs in a dive (wind-up, travel) and under spawn protection (`canChill`). While frozen:
  - **No control** (D7): `frozenInputs` empties the blob's inputs for the controller, combat and the dive, so it cannot move, aim, shoot, crouch or dive.
  - **A puck** (D4): `holdFrozenBlobs` writes the controller's `steerScale` (`slideGrip`, 0.15) and `jumpLocked` at the end of each step, after the dive has set them. The block brakes at 15 % of normal, times the ground's own grip, so frozen ground carries it about ten times further; the ray's push, bumps and blasts all shove it. It does not start sliding down a slope by itself. A roll ends when the blob freezes.
  - **Ways out** (D5): each jump press cracks the block (a jump held at the freeze is no press), and press number `cracksToBreak` (3) breaks it; a hit that lands on it shatters it and stays a normal hit (the match reads combat's `playerHit` events); the timer; fire. After a block ends its blob gathers no chill for `graceSeconds` (0.5 s).
  - **Fire** (D6): ice and fire cancel on a blob. The match puts out a blob that burns and carries chill or a block in the same step (the fire feature's `douseBlob`) and takes the ice off (`releaseBlob`, `melt`). So the ray puts a burning blob out before any chill stays, and the torch or burning ground melts a block without lighting the blob for that touch. Frozen ground alone never freezes a blob.
  - **No damage.**
- **Block look** (owner, D1, D8; the options page's B): a glossy rounded cube in the ice colours around the blob, ink first, see-through fill, the lighter top and two gloss streaks; pinprick pupils inside; a crack line and a drawn 4-unit hop per press; it forms around the blob while the chill builds. Moments (0.5 s): a white pop ring on freezing, chips on a crack, eight shards when it breaks, white steam when it melts. Drawn on the ray layer, above the players and under the breaths.

## Dependencies

- `arena` via `api/arenaApi.ts` (`TerrainGrid`, `getCellInfo`, `isFrozenSample`, `setSampleFrozen`).
- `combat` via `api/combatApi.ts` (`helmetPoint`); combat does not import ice.
- `player` via `api/playerApi.ts` (`PlayerState`, `BlobPoseOverlay`, `endRoll`, `stepPlayers` in the test).
- Shared: `sampleFront`, `rayFan`, `gameTunables` (`ice`, `combat.freezeRay`), `gamePalette.ice`; `pixi.js` in the renderer.

- `src/shared/utils/debugInk.ts`: the ice ink colour (`?debugInk=1` tints it; fire-black-flash diagnosis).

## Decisions

- The cold breath leaves at the knob in front of the freeze ray's dish mouth: `ICE_LOOK.breath.emitterReach` is 20 units along the aim from the helmet point and follows the shared `weaponTube` shape `dish` (weapon-visuals-embellish D1, D8).
- Beam clash (owner, beam-clash D1, the "Steam wall"): a torch flame that meets the ray ends it there. `sprayFreezeRay` takes the `reach` combat worked out (`src/shared/utils/beamClash.ts`) and casts its fan no further, so the ray freezes, chills, pushes and is drawn only up to the meeting point. This feature knows nothing of the other beam.
- Concept (owner, ice-element D1, 2026-11-18, after the options page): a freeze ray, held like the torch, on the same system as fire. Not the freeze rocket, the frost spray or the snow lobber from the page.
- The ice grows only while the ray feeds it (owner, D3), and it grows where the beam points (owner, D8, 2026-11-18, after playing the first build): the first build ran one fire-like front on one feed clock, so on a large surface old edges grew while the owner froze somewhere else. Picked over forgetting waiting edges on release and over growing only edges near the beam. The price, accepted: ice no longer spreads at a steady speed like fire (a big patch's edge moves slower than a small one's); in return a crate always makes the same amount of ice.
- Shared mechanism (D4, code-style F2): fire's front was extracted to `shared/utils/sampleFront`, the torch's ray fan to `shared/utils/rayFan`, each reading the grid as a plain `SampleField`. Fire and ice both call them; nothing is copied. They live in shared, not in the arena or combat, because both features were at their file budget and neither needs anything but the grid's samples. The row-run slabs that were shared here at first (`shared/utils/blockSlabs`) are gone: fire's body and frozen ground are drawn by the arena's region contour (element-overlay-shape D1).
- Frozen behaves like ice and thaws by itself after 8 s (D5, derived from "the same system as fire": a burning piece burns out, a frozen one thaws).
- Fire and ice (owner, D6): spreading fire stops at frozen ground; the torch always thaws it and burns it only if it would have burned anyway.
- Ray look (owner, D9, 2026-11-18, options page): "Cold breath", over a snow stream, a pulse beam and an ice lance, and over the thin tapering beam of the first build ("spray design needs work").
- Sounds (owner picks, element-sounds D5; were placeholders under D2): the ray's start `freeze-start` and a held `freeze-loop`, thawing ground `thaw` (read from `frozenHead`, rate-limited), the torch melting it `steam-hiss`. A blob freezing plays `freeze-solid`, a crack `ice-crack` (softer) and a block breaking `ice-shatter` (owner picks, ice-block-sounds lap; were `clink` placeholders under ice-frozen-blobs D2).
- Frozen blobs (owner, ice-frozen-blobs D1, 2026-11-18, options page https://claude.ai/artifact/JL35sUTupqc3HhsGZykgu9): "Ice block", over "Cold feet" (control kept, no grip; the agent's recommendation) and "Brittle shell" (the next hit costs an extra chunk). It takes control away, unlike the burning blob, which keeps it (fire-burn D3).
- The block slides through the controller's existing motion modifiers, not a new one and not the roll (D4): the roll would bounce, spin and stay steerable. The price: a block on a slope stays put until something shoves it.
- Fire and ice on a blob are cancelled by the match, so neither feature imports the other (D6). Known: the fire's catch pop and cue play for the one step a flame touches a block.
- Starting values (D8): `ice.blob` freeze 0.5 s, crouch × 0.5, frozen 2.5 s, 3 cracks, grip 0.15, grace 0.5 s.
- Starting values (D7, D8): `ice.freezeAreaPerSecond` 3600 units² (a 3 s crate makes a round patch of about 59 units radius inside a slab), `ice.frozenSeconds` 8, beam 180 units long and ±3°, push 300.
